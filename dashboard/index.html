SIH Problem Statement SIH26258

Innovative Mechanized Desludging Models for Varied Climatic Zones and Hilly Terrains

Issued by the Ministry of Jal Shakti under the Clean & Green Technology theme to accelerate Swachh Bharat Mission (Grameen & Urban 2.0) and achieve the national mandate of 100% elimination of hazardous manual cleaning.

01

Severe Inaccessibility in Mountain Alleys

Standard vacuum trucks (3,000–6,000 L) require at least 3.2 meters road width and fail on gradients above 12%. In mountain settlements like Shimla, Aizawl, or Mussoorie, paths are narrower than 1.5m with step staircases and 20°–30° slopes where large trucks physically cannot enter.

02

Atmospheric Pressure Limit at High Altitude

Conventional vacuum tanks depend entirely on atmospheric barometric pressure for suction lift. At 2,200m+ altitude (Leh, Manali), atmospheric pressure drops from 101 kPa to ~75 kPa. This cuts effective suction lift from ~7 meters down to under 4 meters, causing severe cavitation.

03

Sub-Zero Sludge Solidification & Viscosity

In sub-zero Himalayan winters, faecal sludge thickens exponentially. Lipids, fats, and scum freeze into a hardened cake (>3,500 cP). Unheated suction lines freeze and choke within minutes, rendering standard equipment completely inoperable.

04

Foreign Municipal Solid Waste & Choking

Pit latrines and septic tanks in India contain significant volumes of rags, plastics, and sanitary pads. When suction hoses clog mid-way over 50m runs, sanitation workers are forced to dismantle pipes and manually clear blockages—perpetuating unsafe manual intervention.

The Solution: Two-Tier Hub-and-Spoke Architecture

TIER 1 • CRAWLER TRANSIT

TERRA-VAC 450 Mini-Crawler

Ultra-compact rubber-tracked chassis (<900mm wide) climbs 28° slopes, enters tight alleys, and holds 450L intermediate payload with anti-slosh baffles.

PROCESS • SHEAR & HEAT

Inline Cutter & Thermal Jacket

Submersible macerator pulverizes solids at the nozzle tip, while PTC thermal heating jacket lowers apparent viscosity from 2800 cP to 320 cP.

TIER 2 • CONVEYANCE

Road Mother Tanker Transfer

After filling in narrow alleys, the mini-crawler crawls back to the main road and uses high-pressure positive displacement discharge to transfer sludge to the municipal tanker.

Dual rotary chopper blades mounted directly at the intake orifice against a hardened perforated shear plate to eliminate downstream clogging.

  • Operating Speed: 1,200 – 1,800 RPM
  • Particulate Reduction: < 6 mm Maximum Grain

3. Piston Dewatering Cylinder & Ram

Axial Thrust: 12.8 kN

Integrated electric ballscrew piston ram compressing raw slurry against a 150 µm wedge-wire screen. Dewaters in-situ, tripling solids payload capacity and providing positive syringe discharge to the road tanker.

  • Dewatering Compaction: 3.5 bar continuous compaction
  • Solids Concentration: 4.2% Raw ➔ 22.8% Dewatered Cake
  • Water Extraction Ratio: 68% Free Water Returned
  • Service Trip Multiplier: 3.2x Households Serviced
  • Positive Discharge Ejection: 100% Clog-Free Syringe Ejection

4. Progressive Cavity Booster Pump

Positive Displacement

Single-helix tool-steel rotor rotating inside an elastomeric stator. Delivers continuous, pulsation-free pumping irrespective of altitude pressure drops.
