# Equipment Selection Catalog & Configuration Specifications

## 1. Portfolio Architectural Strategy
To ensure operational capability across the full spectrum of topographies, road access widths, and climates, the decision support system evaluates six distinct equipment configurations. 

No single equipment model is optimal for all settings; vehicle-mounted units offer high volumetric pumping throughput but are physically barred from narrow alleys and steep mountain terraces, whereas man-portable and tracked units can reach extreme locations at the cost of lower throughput and higher manual setup requirements.
